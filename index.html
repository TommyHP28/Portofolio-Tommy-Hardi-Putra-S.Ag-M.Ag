Synthesizing classical Islamic theological and philosophical inquiry with contemporary analytical methodologies and digital dissemination.

I am an academic researcher and postgraduate scholar holding a Master of Aqidah and Islamic Philosophy (M.Ag.) from Universitas Islam Negeri Sjech M. Djamil Djambek Bukittinggi, built upon an undergraduate foundation in Hadith Studies (S.Ag.).
